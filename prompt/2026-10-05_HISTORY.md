## UpdatePrompt toast not clickable over issue panel
- Prompt: "Khi mà mở panel của một ticket thì ko ấn được Update now, bị conflict z index sao á, hãy kiểm tra."
- Root cause: not z-index — Radix modal Dialog (Drawer/Dialog) sets body `pointer-events: none`; Sonner toasts inherit it → click-through.
- Fix (shared UI layer): `pointer-events-auto` on toasts in ui/sonner.tsx; `keepOpenOnToastClick` passed as `onInteractOutside` in ui/Drawer.tsx + ui/Dialog.tsx so clicking a toast doesn't close the modal.
